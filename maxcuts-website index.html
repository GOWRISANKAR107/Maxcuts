FAQ

Questions, answered.

What type of videos do you edit?

We specialize in short-form videos, Reels, Shorts, talking-head content, educational videos, project showcases and repurposed clips.

Do I need to provide the footage?

Usually yes. You provide the raw footage, recordings or source material and we handle the editing and packaging. Custom production arrangements can also be discussed.

Can you work with our existing brand style?

Yes. We can work from your existing brand guidelines, references, fonts, colors, editing style and content examples.

How many videos should we start with?

For most brands, 4–6 videos per month is a practical starting point. Once the workflow is stable, production can scale to 8–10 or more.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. If your volume, format or production requirements don't fit a standard package, we'll scope a custom monthly workflow.
